# Sales Performance Dashboard – Power BI

## Overview

This project presents an interactive **Sales Performance Dashboard** developed using **Microsoft Power BI**.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of key sales and profitability metrics and enables users to analyze business performance across different **months, product categories, sub-categories, and regions**.

## Objectives

* Monitor overall sales performance
* Track total sales and total profit
* Analyze total orders and profit margin
* Understand monthly sales trends
* Compare sales performance across categories
* Analyze sales across regions
* Compare sales across sub-categories
* Provide interactive filtering for business analysis

## Tool Used

* **Microsoft Power BI**

## Dashboard Components

### KPI Cards

The dashboard includes four key performance indicators:

* **Total Sales**
* **Total Profit**
* **Total Orders**
* **Profit Margin**

These KPIs provide a quick overview of the overall business performance.

### Monthly Sales Trend

A **Line Chart** is used to visualize sales performance by month.

This helps identify changes and patterns in sales over time.

### Sales by Category

A **Column Chart** displays total sales across different product categories.

This allows users to compare the contribution of each category to overall sales.

### Sales by Region

A **Bar Chart** presents total sales across different regions.

This provides a regional comparison of sales performance.

### Sales by Sub-Category

A **Clustered Bar Chart** is used to analyze sales across product sub-categories.

This helps identify differences in sales contribution at a more detailed product level.

## Interactive Filters

The dashboard provides interactive slicers for:

* **Category**
* **Month**

Users can select specific categories or months to dynamically filter the dashboard and analyze the corresponding sales performance.

## Dashboard Features

* Interactive KPI cards
* Monthly sales trend analysis
* Category-wise sales analysis
* Region-wise sales analysis
* Sub-category-wise sales analysis
* Category filter
* Month filter
* Interactive cross-filtering between visuals
