📊 Day 17 – Monthly Sales Trend Analysis

📌 Project Overview
This project was completed as part of my VEDA Technology Data Analytics Internship – Task 17.
The objective of this task was to analyze retail sales data over a one-year period, summarize sales on a monthly basis, and visualize the sales trend using Microsoft Excel.

🎯 Objectives

- Analyze sales performance month-wise.
- Summarize total sales for each month.
- Identify monthly sales trends and variations.
- Analyze sales performance across different categories.
- Create clear and meaningful visualizations for business insights.
  
📂 Dataset

The dataset contains 500 retail sales records covering January 2025 to December 2025.

🔍 Methodology

1. Data Preparation
- Loaded the retail sales dataset into Excel.
- Verified and formatted the Order Date column.
- Reviewed the dataset for consistency.
- Converted the raw data into an Excel Table.
2. Monthly Sales Analysis
- Created a PivotTable from the sales data.
- Added Order Date to the Rows field.
- Grouped the dates by Month and Year.
- Added Sales to the Values field.
- Calculated Total Sales for each month.
3. Monthly Sales Trend
A Line Chart was created using the monthly sales summary to visualize sales performance throughout 2025.
Chart Title: Monthly Sales Trend – 2025
4. Category Sales Analysis
A separate PivotTable was created to summarize sales by product category.
A Column Chart was then created to compare sales performance across categories.
Chart Title: Sales by Category – 2025
